Our promise

Four things we won't compromise on.

If an item doesn't meet all four, it doesn't go in your box. We'll mark it unavailable instead.

01

Certified farmers only

Every farmer's NPOP or PGS-India certificate is checked on the official portal for crop and expiry. We never mix in uncertified produce.

02

We visit every farm

We walk the fields, check the water source and the input store, and photograph them before any produce reaches you.

03

Tested at a lab

The first harvest from every new farm is tested for pesticide residues at an NABL-accredited lab, with rotating samples after that.

04

Harvested to order

Farmers pick what members have ordered. Nothing sits in a warehouse waiting to be sold. It arrives on your fixed delivery day.

What we're not

Good food takes a little planning.

We made some choices on purpose. They're why we can promise what we promise.

i.

10-minute delivery

Speed means stock sitting in dark stores. We'd rather harvest to order and deliver on fixed days.

ii.

"100% organic" stickers

We won't make claims we can't back up. We show certificates and lab reports instead.

iii.

Substitutes

If no certified farmer has an item this week, it's marked unavailable. We never swap in uncertified produce.

iv.

Open to everyone, all at once

We grow one group of families at a time, so quality never slips as we grow.

Questions

Good to know.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. The waitlist is free and you don't commit to anything. We'll message you when we're ready to open in your area, and you decide then.

Do I have to buy a box?

No. You can order single items by the kg, half kg, bunch or dozen, choose a ready seasonal box, or do both in the same order.

When are you launching?

We're verifying farms and certificates now. We'll open to the first 50 families only once supply is proven. Waitlist members hear first.

Which areas will you deliver to?

We'll start with a few areas of Surat so routes stay short and produce stays fresh. Where we start depends on where waitlist families live, so telling us your area really helps.

What does "certified organic" mean here?

It means the produce comes from farmers certified under India's NPOP or PGS-India systems, and we check each certificate on the official portal. A lab test is a separate check that we do as well. It doesn't replace certification.

Why invite-only?

Certified-organic supply is limited. Opening in small groups means we never promise more than our farmers can grow, and we never fill the gap with uncertified produce.
